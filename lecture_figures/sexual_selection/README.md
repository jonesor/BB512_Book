# Lecture 18: Sexual selection and mating systems

Figures for the BB512 lecture "Sexual selection and mating systems"
(Keynote deck "BB512 - 18 - Sexual selection v2"). Reading: Neal, chapter
"Sexual selection and mating systems".

| Script | Slide | Layout | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| `01_anisogamy_disruptive.R` | Anisogamy: two gamete sizes | `half` | Schematic (disruptive selection on gamete size) |
| `02_bateman_mates.R` | Bateman (1948): who goes unmated? | `half` | Empirical, digitised from Neal's redrawn figure |
| `03_bateman_gradient.R` | The Bateman gradient | `half` | Empirical, digitised from Neal's redrawn figure |
| `04_pinniped_harems.R` | Why are males often bigger? | `half` | Empirical, digitised from Neal (Alexander et al. 1979) |
| `05_widowbird.R` | Testing female choice: widowbirds | `takeaway` | Empirical, Neal's table of Andersson (1982) |

Render from the repository root with
`Rscript lecture_figures/render_all.R sexual_selection`.

Other assets: `images/` holds the photos (`p01`–`p09`) and `videos/` the video clips
(`v1`–`v4`) used in the deck. `keynote/` holds the copies of figures `01`–`05` that
were placed in the Keynote deck (exported 24 Oct 2026).
